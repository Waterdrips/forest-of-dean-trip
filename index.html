🌲 Forest of Dean · 26–30 Oct

Dog-friendly walks, lunches, dinners and towns. Distances are rough road estimates from the cabins; tap Directions for the real time.

🏡 Directions back to cabin Tips Good pairings

Good pairings

  • Symonds Yat: the Rock and the Biblins loop, then the Saracens Head, or 15 min on to Ross-on-Wye.
  • Staunton: the Buckstone, then the White Horse, or 10 min on to Monmouth.
  • Central forest: the Sculpture Trail, Beechenhurst Café, Cannop Ponds, and dinner at the Speech House.
  • Lakes: Mallards Pike, stopping at New Fancy View on the way, then the Wild Boar Coffee café at Parkend.
  • Lower Wye: the Devil's Pulpit at Tintern, then lunch and the castle in Chepstow (10 min).
  • Goodrich: Goodrich Castle, then lunch at the Inn on the Wye (Kerne Bridge, just down the hill), then Ross.
  • Lydney side: Dean Forest Railway and Taurus Crafts, then a flat walk at Lydney Harbour.
  • Rainy day: Clearwell Caves (take turns, dogs stay on the surface) and Perrygrove Railway, both 5–10 min away.

Tips

  • It's half-term. Book dinners early, and ask for a quiet corner table for the dog.
  • Clocks went back on 25 Oct. It's dark by about 4:45pm. Pack head torches if you walk to the New Inn.
  • Wear boots: leaves and mud make the paths slippery, especially carrying babies.
  • Keep the dog on a short lead in Wye Valley fields; sheep and cattle graze there, and the Forest has free-roaming sheep and wild boar.
  • Skip Puzzlewood: it allows no dogs and no pushchairs.
  • Places marked "Ring ahead" are from listings that weren't confirmed; check dogs are still welcome.
  • Emergencies: 999. Non-urgent medical: NHS 111. Vets and supplies are in the Essentials filter.
